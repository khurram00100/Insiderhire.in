1. What InsiderHire Is

InsiderHire is a marketplace platform operated at www.insiderhire.in that connects international students ("Job Seekers" or "Freshers") with local workers ("Referrers") who can provide internal job referrals for odd jobs including hotel staff, kitchen staff, warehouse workers, retail assistants, security guards, delivery riders, cleaners, and labour jobs.

InsiderHire is a marketplace only. We are not an employer, employment agency, or recruitment firm. We do not guarantee job placement. We facilitate connections and hold escrow payments.

4. Fees and Payments

Platform Access Fee · $10 USD
Charged to Job Seekers only. One-time, non-refundable. Grants access to browse and request referrers. Payable in INR equivalent via Razorpay.
Referral Escrow · $500 USD
Held in escrow upon Job Seeker's payment after Referrer accepts request. Payable in INR equivalent via Razorpay. Funds are locked until job confirmation or 14-day auto-release.
Platform Commission · $100 USD (20%)

Referrer Registration · Free
Referrers do not pay any fee to join. They earn $400 per successful referral.

All USD amounts are converted to INR at the prevailing exchange rate at time of payment. Exchange rate fluctuations are borne by the user.

5. Escrow Flow and Obligations

1.Job Seeker sends referral request. Referrer accepts.
2.Job Seeker deposits $500 escrow. Funds locked immediately.
3.Referrer's full contact details are revealed. Chat unlocks.
4.Referrer must submit the job application within 14 days of escrow payment.
5.On job confirmation: Job Seeker uploads first payslip. Referrer uploads matching payslip. Admin verifies both within 24–48 hours.
6.Escrow releases: $400 to Referrer, $100 to InsiderHire.

Referrers who accept payment and fail to submit referrals are in breach of these terms and may face account suspension, chargeback to their linked account, and legal action.
