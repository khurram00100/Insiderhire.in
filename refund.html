1. Platform Access Fee · $10

This fee is strictly non-refundable. It covers the cost of account setup, identity verification infrastructure, and platform access. It is charged at signup and cannot be reversed regardless of subsequent account activity.

This applies even if you:

·Never request a referral
·Close your account immediately after payment
·Fail to find a suitable referrer
·Do not use all 5 weekly requests during the 60-day period
·Pay the renewal fee before the 60-day period expires
📅 The $10 access fee is valid for 60 days from the date of payment. After 60 days, you must pay again to continue sending referral requests. You will receive a reminder 7 days before expiry.

2. Escrow Payment · $500

✅ Full Refund Conditions (you receive $485)
✓Referrer fails to submit referral within 14 days of your escrow payment
✓Referrer is found to have misrepresented their employment status (confirmed by admin)
✓Platform is unable to facilitate the connection due to a technical error on our side
✓Admin rules in your favour following a formal dispute

Refund amount: $500 − $15 processing fee = $485 returned to original Razorpay payment method.

✗ No Refund Conditions
✗Referral was submitted but you did not get the job (job placement is not guaranteed)
✗You changed your mind after escrow payment
✗Employer rejected your application after referral was submitted
✗You provided false or misleading information in your profile
✗Your account was suspended due to violation of Terms of Service
✗You contacted the referrer or employer outside the platform

3. How to Request a Refund

1
Go to Wallet · Dashboard → Escrow Wallet → Active Locks → "Request Refund" button on the relevant escrow.
2
Admin review · Our team reviews the request, chat logs, and referral submission records within 2 business days.
3
Decision · You receive an email with the admin's decision and reason within 2 business days.
4
Refund processed · If approved, Razorpay processes the refund to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Bank transfer times vary.

4. Processing Fee

A flat $15 USD (INR equivalent) processing fee is deducted from all approved escrow refunds. This covers Razorpay reversal costs, admin review time, and fraud monitoring. You receive $485 USD equivalent.

5. Referrer Earnings · No Clawback After Release

Once escrow has been released to a Referrer's wallet following successful verification, that amount cannot be reversed or reclaimed by InsiderHire unless fraud is proven through legal process. Job Seekers should raise disputes before escrow is released.

6. Chargebacks

Initiating a chargeback through your bank or card provider without first raising a dispute through InsiderHire's platform is a violation of these terms. We reserve the right to permanently ban accounts involved in fraudulent chargebacks and pursue recovery through legal means.
