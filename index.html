Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025  ·  Effective immediately

📋 InsiderHire ("we", "us", "our") operates at www.insiderhire.in. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights over it. By using InsiderHire you agree to this policy.

1. Who We Are

InsiderHire is a job referral marketplace connecting international students seeking odd jobs (hotel staff, kitchen staff, warehouse workers, retail assistants, security guards, delivery riders, cleaners, and labour jobs) with verified local workers who can refer them to employers.

Contact: privacy@insiderhire.in  ·  www.insiderhire.in

2. Data We Collect

Account Data
Full name, email address, password (hashed), role (fresher/referrer), date of registration.
Profile Data
Phone number, location, nationality, age, gender, work eligibility, visa status, employment history, skills, languages, LinkedIn URL, profile photo, CV/resume documents.
Payment Data
Transaction IDs, Razorpay payment references, escrow amounts, payout details (bank account/UPI for referrers). We do NOT store card numbers · Razorpay handles all card data under PCI-DSS compliance.
Usage & Location Data
IP address, browser type, pages visited, search queries within the platform, approximate geolocation (when you grant permission for radius-based referrer search).
Verification Documents
Payslips, passport copies, visa documents, employment verification documents · stored encrypted in private Supabase Storage.
Communications
Messages exchanged between users on the platform, support tickets submitted.

3. Why We Collect It

→Service delivery · matching job seekers to referrers, processing escrow payments, releasing funds after job confirmation.
→Identity verification · confirming referrers work where they claim, preventing fraud.
→Payment processing · via Razorpay for escrow, platform fees, and payouts.
→Location-based search · showing referrers within your chosen radius (10/50/100/500km). We do not track your location continuously.
→Legal compliance · maintaining records required by tax authorities and payment regulations.
→Safety · detecting fraud, resolving disputes, enforcing our Terms of Service.

4. Information Masking

Before an escrow payment is confirmed, referrer personal information is masked on all user-facing screens:

  • · Name: Shamsheer Khan → Sham**** Kh**
  • · Phone: 6303716492 → ********92
  • · Email: john@gmail.com → jo***@gmail.com
  • · Company, workplace, and address: fully hidden

After a confirmed $500 escrow deposit, full contact details are revealed to the paying fresher only.

4A. Pre-Chat Data & Restrictions

InsiderHire operates a Pre-Chat verification system between freshers and referrers. The following rules apply to all pre-chat communications:

  • ✗ Phone numbers may not be shared in any chat
  • ✗ Exact physical addresses may not be shared
  • ✗ Free-text personal messages are not permitted in Pre-Chat
  • ✓ Only pre-typed system messages are allowed in Pre-Chat
  • ✓ All pre-chat messages are recorded and stored in our database
  • ✓ Full chat (post-escrow) messages are also stored for dispute resolution

Pre-chat message data is retained for 2 years for dispute resolution and platform safety purposes. Users may request deletion subject to legal retention requirements.

5. Who We Share Data With

→Razorpay · payment processing. Their privacy policy applies to payment data: razorpay.com/privacy
→Supabase · database and file storage infrastructure (EU-based servers).
→Google Maps · location and mapping data when you use radius filtering.
→Between users · after escrow payment, your contact details are shared with your matched referrer/fresher.
→Law enforcement · when legally required to prevent fraud or comply with court orders.

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for commercial purposes.

6. Data Retention

·Active account data: retained while your account is active.
·Payment records: retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance.
·Messages and chat logs: retained for 2 years for dispute resolution.
·Verification documents: deleted within 90 days of account deletion request.
·Deleted accounts: anonymised within 30 days, payment records retained per above.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your country, you have the right to:

Access
Request a copy of your data
Rectification
Correct inaccurate information
Erasure
Delete your account and data
Portability
Export your data as CSV
Objection
Object to specific processing
Withdraw consent
Cancel location access anytime

To exercise any right: privacy@insiderhire.in. We respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

We use only essential session cookies required for authentication. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies. Your browser settings control cookie acceptance.

9. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Payment data handled by Razorpay under PCI-DSS Level 1. Verification documents stored in private, access-controlled storage. We conduct regular security reviews.

10. Changes to This Policy

We will notify registered users by email 14 days before any material change. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

Questions? Contact us at privacy@insiderhire.in or write to: InsiderHire, www.insiderhire.in
